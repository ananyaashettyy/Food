MODULE 1: FOUNDATIONS
1. What is Monitoring?
Definition
Monitoring is the process of collecting predefined metrics, logs, and events from systems and checking them against predefined thresholds or rules.
Monitoring is primarily used to determine whether known problems are occurring.

Characteristics of Monitoring

Reactive approach
Threshold based
Focuses on known issues
Uses dashboards and alerts
Depends on predefined conditions


Example
Alert when:
Plain Text1CPU Utilization > 90% for 5 minutesShow more lines
or
Plain Text1Disk Usage > 85%Show more lines

Main Question Answered

Is something wrong right now?


Monitoring in One Line

Monitoring tells you WHEN something you expected to break actually breaks.


2. What is Observability?
Definition
Observability is the ability to understand the internal state of a system by analyzing the telemetry data generated by that system.
Telemetry includes:

Metrics
Logs
Traces

Unlike monitoring, observability helps investigate unknown problems.

Characteristics

Proactive
Investigative
Root-cause focused
Handles unknown issues
Supports ad-hoc analysis


Main Question Answered

Why is something happening?


Observability in One Line

Observability tells you WHY something broke, even if you never expected it.
