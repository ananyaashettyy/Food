Module 1: Introduction to IT Service Management (ITSM)
1. What is IT Service Management (ITSM)?
Definition
IT Service Management (ITSM) is the practice of designing, delivering, managing, and improving IT services to meet business needs.
Purpose

Align IT with business objectives.
Deliver value to customers.
Improve service quality.
Ensure efficient use of IT resources.

2. What is the ITIL Framework?
Definition
ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library) is the most widely used framework for implementing ITSM best practices.
Objectives

Standardize IT processes.
Improve service quality.
Reduce operational risks.
Enhance customer satisfaction.

Benefits
✔ Consistent service delivery
✔ Better governance
✔ Reduced downtime
✔ Improved business alignment
✔ Continual improvement
