# DWP & Service Desk Concepts – Detailed Notes

# 1. Introduction to Digital Workplace (DWP)

## 1.1 What is a Digital Workplace?

A **Digital Workplace (DWP)** is a technology-enabled environment that allows employees to communicate, collaborate, access applications, and perform work from anywhere using digital tools and platforms.

### Definition

A digital workplace integrates people, processes, and technology to improve productivity, collaboration, and employee experience.

### Evolution of Digital Workplace

* Traditional office-based work
* Intranet and email systems
* Cloud-based collaboration platforms
* Remote and hybrid work environments
* AI-driven digital workplaces

### Key Characteristics

* Anywhere access
* Cloud-enabled services
* Real-time collaboration
* Secure device management
* Automated workflows

***

# 5. Virtual Desktop Infrastructure (VDI) and DaaS

## 5.1 What are the Benefits of VDI?

All desktops managed centrally.

### Improved Security

Data remains in the data center.

### Reduced Hardware Requirements

Thin clients can be used.

### Business Continuity

Access desktops from anywhere.

### Better Compliance

Centralized monitoring and auditing.

***

## 5.2 How Does VDI Differ from DaaS?

| Feature        | VDI                | DaaS              |
| -------------- | ------------------ | ----------------- |
| Ownership      | Organization       | Service Provider  |
| Hosting        | On-Premises        | Cloud             |
| Infrastructure | Managed by Company | Managed by Vendor |
| Cost Model     | Capital Expense    | Subscription      |
| Scalability    | Limited            | Highly Scalable   |

### Examples

#### VDI

* Citrix Virtual Apps and Desktops
* VMware Horizon

#### DaaS

* Azure Virtual Desktop
* Amazon WorkSpaces

### Step 2: Categorization

# 9. Performance and Metrics

## 9.1 Key Service Desk Metrics

### First Call Resolution (FCR)

Percentage of incidents resolved during first contact.

**Formula:**

```text
FCR = (Resolved on First Contact / Total Calls) × 100
```

***

### Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR)

Average time required to resolve incidents.

**Formula:**

```text
MTTR = Total Resolution Time / Number of Incidents
```

***

### SLA Compliance

Measures adherence to agreed service levels.

**Examples**

* Response within 1 hour
* Resolution within 8 hours

***
